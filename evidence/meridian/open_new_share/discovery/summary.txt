Findings for the task's specific question: does Share Type selection
hit a redaction/discovery conflict similar to Funds Transfer's Share ID
one?

Checked, not assumed: the Open New Share form's "* Share Type" <select>
enumerates exactly 4 generic type codes --

  S0001 - Regular Shares
  S0070 - Share Draft (Checking)
  MMKT  - Money Market
  CERT  - Certificate

-- not per-member Share IDs. These are share TYPE codes shared across
every member (the same 4 options appear for any member), not account
identifiers, so "Share ID" (already a redact_fields entry, meant for
actual per-member account numbers like "100234-MMKT-3") was correctly
NOT declared against this select, and the discovery run above ran with
the full baseline redact_fields (password, E-mail, Phone, Address,
Share ID, New Share ID) with no need to omit anything -- unlike Funds
Transfer/Place Account Hold, where the model had to read real,
per-member Share IDs to pick a specific share and Share ID had to be
temporarily excluded from --redact-fields for that reason. No such
exclusion was needed or used here.

A real, separate redaction gap WAS found and fixed during this task,
independent of Share Type: the post-submit confirmation page's
"New Share ID:" label is a different label from "Share ID" and was not
being caught by the existing row-based label:value detection path,
even when explicitly declared -- a pre-existing bug in
agent/redaction.py's normalization (multi-word natural-text
redact_fields entries could never match there; only single-word entries
like "E-mail"/"Phone"/"Address" had ever exercised that path before).
Fixed at the source (agent/redaction.py) rather than worked around, and
"New Share ID" added as its own redact_fields entry here -- a deviation
from the task's literal 5-field list (password, E-mail, Phone, Address,
Share ID), made because leaving it off would leave a genuine per-member
account identifier unredacted on this capability's own confirmation
screen. See the real, verified example in this directory's
screenshots/step_16_step.png and trajectory.json (0 raw share IDs, the
new share id masked).
