--- Real, non-injected minimum-deposit rejection ---
Two real minimum-deposit rejections captured live (not the ?inject=
mechanism -- this is the app's own natural business-rule validation):

  GET .../open-share, Share Type = Regular Shares, Initial Deposit = $1.00
  -> POST .../open-share/review ->

    OPEN NEW SHARE
    The request could not be validated:
    A minimum opening deposit of $5.00 is required.

  GET .../open-share, Share Type = Certificate, Initial Deposit = $50.00
  -> POST .../open-share/review ->

    OPEN NEW SHARE
    The request could not be validated:
    Certificates require a minimum opening deposit of $500.00.

Different share types enforce different minimums (at least Certificate
does; Regular Shares' minimum is $5.00). Both messages share the
substring "minimum opening deposit", which is what MINIMUM_DEPOSIT_NOT_MET's
detection matches on -- deliberately not enumerating a separate outcome
per share type, since the task asked for the general "below whatever
minimum is enforced" case, not a full per-type minimum-deposit matrix.
Replay-tested for real against the $1.00/Regular Shares case; see
../replays/minimum_deposit_not_met/.
