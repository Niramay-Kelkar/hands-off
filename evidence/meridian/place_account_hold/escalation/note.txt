Real, end-to-end escalation test for meridian_place_account_hold,
run via a driver script mirroring
.github/scripts/check_escalation_lifecycle.py's CDP-attach pattern
(agent.replay --headless with AGENT_CDP_PORT set; a second Playwright
process attaches to the SAME live browser to play the human operator's
part; the real agent.operator_console /resume/<run_id> endpoint is used
for both resumes, unmodified).

Input: operator_id=teller1, member_id=100234, share=100234-CERT-27.

1. Risk-gate pause (owner=human, pause_reason=risk_confirmation_required,
   step=0) -- resumed immediately, as with any mutating capability.

2. Step 12 (the "Continue" click on the hold form) executes: the ACT
   phase succeeds (the click really happens), but the CHECK phase's
   checkpoint -- element_visible(button "Apply Hold") OR
   outcome_match(INVALID_CREDENTIALS, VALIDATION_ERROR) -- fails,
   because teller1 lands on the "SUPERVISOR OVERRIDE REQUIRED"
   rejection page instead. Because step 12 carries this capability's
   step_overrides (retry: 0, then: escalate, on every trigger), the
   engine escalates immediately rather than retrying. Confirmed in
   sessions.db: status=paused, current_step_id=12,
   pause_reason=on_checkpoint_failure, with a real screenshot
   (screenshots/step_12_on_checkpoint_failure.png) showing the exact
   rejection text captured live in exploration/recon_output.txt.

3. Real human recovery, in the SAME live browser session (not a fresh
   one -- same CDP-attached page, same cookies/session): clicked
   "Return to previous screen", signed off teller1, signed on as
   super1, re-navigated Place Account Hold -> searched/selected member
   100234 -> re-filled the hold form (same share/reason/notes) -> Continue.
   This lands on the real CONFIRM ACCOUNT HOLD review screen, i.e. it
   makes step 12's ORIGINAL checkpoint condition (Apply Hold visible)
   true, without the engine having re-run step 12's action itself --
   the human account for the actual retry of the submission, exactly
   once, by hand.

4. POSTed /resume/<run_id>. The engine's CHECK-phase retry re-evaluates
   the SAME checkpoint (never re-invokes step 12's action, per the
   ACT/SETTLE/CHECK split -- see CLAUDE.md's Safety section), finds
   "Apply Hold" now visible, step 12 completes, and execution continues
   automatically to step 13 (click "Apply Hold") -- the automation
   itself performs the actual mutating POST, not the human. Result:
   status=success, hold_confirmation=CN480446 (a real hold, confirmed
   live on MERIDIAN CORE).

This is the answer to why Place Hold's handoff differs from Funds
Transfer's: Transfer's escalation trigger (an unrecognized dialog) is
fixed by the human WITHOUT changing who's signed on -- dismiss the
dialog, resume, the automation's own next click continues normally.
Place Hold's trigger is a genuine privilege wall baked into the signed-
on identity itself, so the human's recovery necessarily includes a
sign-off/sign-on cycle inside the SAME session before the automation
can complete the very same in-flight capability run.
